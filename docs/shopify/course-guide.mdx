# Shopify Course Guide

## Audience and setup

For aspiring merchants, store operators, marketers, support staff, and junior theme practitioners. You need a modern browser and an authorized Shopify development/trial store or a written simulation. Theme coding is optional; basic HTML/CSS is useful.

Shopify features, plans, payments, taxes, markets, and carrier services vary by country, plan, and eligibility. This course teaches decisions and controls; always validate legal, tax, accessibility, privacy, product-safety, consumer-protection, and payment obligations with qualified local advisers. Never represent a learning sandbox as a live business.

## Assessment blueprint

| Competence | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Plan and governance | Customer, offer, KPI, role and risk brief |
| Catalog | Accurate products, variants, collections, media and metadata |
| Experience | Accessible theme, navigation, content and mobile test |
| Commerce readiness | Payments, tax, shipping, policy and test-order evidence |
| Operations | Order, refund, inventory, support and incident procedures |
| Improvement | Analytics interpretation and prioritized experiment |

Pass the knowledge check at 80% and capstone at 70/100, including critical privacy, payment and launch-safety criteria. Course v1.0, published 31 August 2026; review by 31 August 2027 or after material Shopify changes.
