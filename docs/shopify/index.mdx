# Shopify Store Operations

**SKW-SH-101 · Beginner to intermediate · 14 hours · Self-paced**

Build an evidence-led store from business requirements through launch and daily operations. The course covers configuration, catalog, content, themes, payments, shipping, orders, analytics, accessibility, privacy, and controlled improvement.

## Outcomes

You will be able to plan a viable store, configure roles and settings, create a reliable catalog, design an accessible storefront, prepare payments/tax/shipping policies, operate orders and support, interpret commerce data, and execute a controlled launch.

| Module | Focus | Time |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Business, customer and store plan | 90 min |
| 2 | Administration, identity and settings | 90 min |
| 3 | Catalog and merchandising | 120 min |
| 4 | Theme, content and experience | 120 min |
| 5 | Checkout, payments, tax and delivery | 120 min |
| 6 | Operations, analytics and growth | 105 min |
| Labs + assessment | Practice and capstone | 195 min |

<div className="checkpoint"><strong>Safety:</strong> Work in a development, trial, or password-protected store. Use test payments only. Never enter real card or customer data for coursework.</div>
