# Administration, Identity and Core Settings

**Outcome:** Configure store identity, roles, domains, notifications and core settings with least privilege and traceable ownership.

Use unique accounts, MFA, and role-based access. Give staff and collaborators only required permissions; review access on role change and periodically. Do not share owner credentials. Inventory apps and themes, their permissions, data access, owner, business purpose, cost, and removal plan.

Confirm store name, legal entity, contact details, time zone, units, default currency, markets, locations, order identifiers, customer account choice, notification sender, and domain/DNS ownership. Changing currency, domain, or market design later can affect reporting, SEO, integrations, and customer expectations—use a decision record.

Domains require correct DNS and renewal ownership. Email sending needs aligned sender authentication and monitoring. Use a password-protected storefront until launch acceptance is complete. Configure privacy and customer-data options according to applicable law and approved policy.

Backups are broader than theme code. Identify how you will recover product/catalog exports, theme versions, pages/policies, app configurations, media, customer/order records allowed by platform and law, and operational procedures. Test at least one recovery path.
