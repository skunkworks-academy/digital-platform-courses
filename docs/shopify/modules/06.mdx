# Operations, Analytics and Improvement

**Outcome:** Operate a daily control cycle and convert trustworthy data into a bounded improvement experiment.

Define order states and owners from payment through fulfilment, delivery, cancellation, return, refund, fraud review and dispute. Use checklists for exception-prone work: address changes, split shipments, substitutions, high-risk orders, damaged returns, chargebacks, and goodwill refunds. Never request full payment credentials over support channels.

Reconcile orders, payouts, fees, refunds, taxes and fulfilment records. Perform inventory counts and record adjustment reasons. Monitor failed payments, unfulfilled aging, delivery exceptions, low stock, returns, support backlog, app/theme changes, domain/notification health, and security access.

Analytics needs definitions. Sessions, conversion, attribution and customer counts differ across tools and consent states. Record source, scope, time zone, filters, calculation and known limitations. Segment carefully and avoid identifying small groups unnecessarily.

For improvement, state a hypothesis, target segment/page, primary metric, guardrails, minimum observation plan, owner, and rollback. Change one important factor where possible. Guardrails might include margin, refund rate, accessibility defects, page speed, support contact, and complaint rate. Avoid deceptive experimentation.
