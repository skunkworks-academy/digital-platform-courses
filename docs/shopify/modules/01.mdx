# Business, Customer and Store Plan

**Outcome:** Produce a one-page store brief connecting target customer, offer, economics, journey, risks, and measures.

Start with the customer problem and buying context. Define a primary segment, job-to-be-done, objections, trust needs, accessibility needs, and post-purchase expectations. Translate the offer into products, variants, bundles/subscriptions if applicable, content, support, and fulfilment requirements.

Estimate unit economics: selling price minus product cost, packaging, payment fees, fulfilment, shipping subsidy, returns, discounts, taxes borne by the business, and allocated acquisition cost. Contribution margin—not revenue—funds operations. Model base, downside, and upside cases.

Map the journey: discovery → product evaluation → cart → checkout → confirmation → fulfilment → delivery → support/return → repeat purchase. At each stage record the customer question, evidence of trust, failure mode, owner, and measure.

Choose a small KPI set: qualified sessions, product-view-to-cart, checkout conversion, average order value, contribution margin, return/refund rate, on-time fulfilment, support contact rate, repeat purchase, and accessibility/experience defects. Respect consent and data minimization.
