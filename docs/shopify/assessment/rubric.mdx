# Shopify Capstone Rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Meets standard | Critical? |
|---|---:|---|:---:|
| Strategy and economics | 10 | Customer, offer, journey, contribution and KPIs are coherent | No |
| Governance and configuration | 10 | Least privilege, ownership, settings, apps and recovery are controlled | Yes |
| Catalog and inventory | 15 | Complete, accurate, accessible and operationally maintainable | No |
| Storefront experience | 15 | Mobile, accessible, performant, transparent and tested | Yes |
| Transaction and legal readiness | 20 | Test-only payments; tax/legal boundaries; shipping/policies validated | Yes |
| Order operations and reconciliation | 15 | Lifecycle and exceptions are accurate, traceable and reconciled | Yes |
| Launch and improvement | 10 | Blockers, approval, rollback, measures and ethical experiment are clear | No |
| Evidence integrity | 5 | Authentic, redacted, mapped and reproducible | Yes |

Use 0/50/70/85/100% performance anchors. Pass at 70/100 and meet every critical criterion. Any live payment data, unauthorized publication, or unnecessary customer data is an automatic critical failure pending remediation.
