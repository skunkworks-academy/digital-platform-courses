# Shopify Capstone — Controlled Store Launch

## Scenario

Launch a password-protected demo store for a South African specialty-goods brand with five products, two locations, domestic shipping, one promotional offer, and an international expansion proposal. Use synthetic identities and test payments.

## Deliverables

1. Customer/offer brief, unit-economics model, journey and KPI definitions.
2. Least-privilege role/app register and core-setting decision record.
3. Five accurate products with governed variants, media, inventory and collections.
4. Accessible mobile-first theme with navigation, content, policies and performance evidence.
5. Payment, tax-advice, shipping, privacy, return and support readiness records.
6. Successful, declined, fulfilment, cancellation, partial/full refund, notification and reconciliation test evidence.
7. Launch checklist with owner, pass/fail, blocker, rollback and approval.
8. Seven-day operations checklist and incident escalation path.
9. Analytics interpretation plus one ethical, bounded improvement experiment.

Estimated effort: 120 minutes after labs. The store must remain non-public unless separately authorized through a real operational launch process.
