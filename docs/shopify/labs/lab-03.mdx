# Lab 3 — Test Order to Refund

**Time:** 60 minutes

Enable a supported test-payment method. Configure a simple domestic shipping rate and a free-shipping threshold. Create test carts below and above the threshold and for one unsupported address. Complete a successful and declined test transaction. Fulfil the successful order, add tracking, verify customer notifications, partially refund one line, restock according to policy, then cancel/refund a separate test order.

Reconcile the order totals, discount, shipping, recorded tax treatment, refund, and inventory movement. Document what must be validated by a tax professional and what is controlled by the payment provider/platform.

<div className="evidence"><strong>Evidence:</strong> test-mode proof, cases/results, redacted order timeline, notification checklist, reconciliation, inventory adjustment, exception log, and launch blocker list. No real payment or personal data.</div>
