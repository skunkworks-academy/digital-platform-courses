# Lab 1 — Store Foundation and Catalog

**Time:** 60 minutes · **Environment:** development/trial store

Create a one-page store brief for a three-product ethical stationery brand. Configure safe core settings and document market, currency, location, role, domain, notification, privacy, and backup decisions. Add three products, at least one with variants. Use unique SKUs, weights, costs, prices, inventory rules, categories/tags, accurate descriptions, and meaningful alt text.

Create one manual and one rule-based collection. Test a product entering and leaving the rule-based collection by changing its governed attribute. Explain your overselling and safety-stock choices.

<div className="evidence"><strong>Evidence:</strong> redacted settings matrix, staff/app access register, product and variant export or screenshots, collection rules/test, inventory rationale, and data-quality checklist.</div>
