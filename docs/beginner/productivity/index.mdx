# Microsoft 365 & Excel Productivity Essentials

**SKW-BEG-M365-101 · Beginner · 8–10 hours · Self-paced · Proposed US$50**

Build a practical office-workflow portfolio: organized files, reliable spreadsheets, clear emails, safe collaboration and accurate reporting.

**Who this is for:** First-time office employees, job seekers, administrative assistants and small-business owners.

**Prerequisites:** A computer or mobile browser, spreadsheet application (Excel web/desktop or compatible alternative), and fictional practice files. A paid Microsoft 365 subscription is not required for written simulations.

## Six learning outcomes

1. Microsoft 365, accounts and cloud file basics: A colleague can identify the authoritative file, explain whether it is local or cloud-based and determine who may edit it.
2. Professional documents and email communication: The artefacts communicate verified information, clear next actions and accessible structure without disclosing restricted content.
3. Excel worksheets, data types and reference skills: Each transaction multiplies its quantity and price correctly, formulas copy predictably, and input fields are typed consistently.
4. Excel formulas, functions and quality checks: Every aggregate is independently reproducible and errors, blanks and duplicate handling are documented.
5. Sorting, filtering, visuals and basic reporting: The table retains data integrity after sorting, the chart states units and denominators, and conclusions match the evidence.
6. Teams collaboration, sharing permissions and productivity workflow: A governed reporting workflow can be reproduced safely, with explicit permissions, approval, logging and a rollback plan.

## How to learn

Complete the before-course diagnostic, read the course guide and modules in order, perform all three practical labs, attempt the knowledge assessment and prepare an evidence-led capstone. Take the diagnostic again at the end to gauge improvement.
