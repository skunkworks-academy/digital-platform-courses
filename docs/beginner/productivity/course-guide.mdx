# Course guide

**Course:** SKW-BEG-M365-101 — Microsoft 365 & Excel Productivity Essentials. **Level:** Beginner. **Duration:** 8–10 hours.

## Before starting

A computer or mobile browser, spreadsheet application (Excel web/desktop or compatible alternative), and fictional practice files. A paid Microsoft 365 subscription is not required for written simulations.

Use only fabricated practice datasets, accounts you control, or systems you are explicitly authorised to access. Do not upload private invoices, access credentials, real payroll or personal identity documents.

## Learning workflow

1. Complete the five-question ungraded baseline diagnostic on the course home page.
2. Work through six 45–60 minute lessons, including the example, guided activity and lesson self-check.
3. Complete all three hands-on labs; record one valid case, one negative case and a reproducible evidence pack.
4. Attempt the 12-question knowledge assessment. **10/12** is the smallest passing integer at an 80% standard.
5. Complete the capstone and seek feedback against the weighted rubric: **70/100**, plus all safety-critical criteria.
6. Repeat the diagnostic and compare it with the initial result; write a skills-gap improvement plan.

## Acceptable evidence

Your work may be a workbook, PDF, Markdown report or accessible simulation. Record objective, inputs, procedure, expected result, actual result, screenshots or redacted values, corrective actions and reflection. Browser progress is local and self-reported: official Academy completion requires separate authenticated assessment and moderation.

## Accessibility and help

All content has a written path; no videos, expensive subscriptions or live production systems are required. Use real heading styles and descriptive chart labels. For accessibility accommodations or course questions, contact [Academy training support](mailto:training@skunkworks.africa).
