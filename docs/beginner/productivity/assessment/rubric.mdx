# Assessment and moderation guide

**Knowledge check:** minimum 10 correct from 12 (meeting or exceeding 80%). **Applied capstone:** 70 of 100, and every critical privacy/accuracy criterion must pass.

| Evidence criterion | Weight | Common failure to correct |
|---|---:|---|
| Outcomes and planning | 15 | Scope unclear or unmeasurable |
| Technical accuracy | 25 | Incorrect formula, corrupted row or unsafe sharing |
| Deliverable quality | 25 | No accessible report, no proof of source accuracy |
| Positive and negative testing | 20 | No failure injection or recovery |
| Governance and reflection | 15 | No owner, approval or improvement plan |

Every lab should record inputs, outputs, expected results, observed behaviour and a corrective note. Formative diagnostics and browser progress are self-reported. A competent independent reviewer moderates capstones, provides criterion-specific feedback and allows up to two guided retries using a comparable new scenario. Apply minimal, access-controlled evidence retention.
