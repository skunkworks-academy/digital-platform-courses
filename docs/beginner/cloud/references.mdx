## Notes on prices and vendor claims

The pricing numbers in course scenarios are explicitly fictional examples, **not live provider quotes**. Always check current regions, tax, support, networking and free-tier restrictions with the relevant provider. This is independent training and does not confer an AWS or Microsoft credential.
