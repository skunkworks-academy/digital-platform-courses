# Capstone: Plan a small-business cloud service

## Stakeholder scenario

A fictional training business needs a marketing website, a private file library, low-cost usage and basic recovery capability. It has 15 staff, a part-time administrator and a total illustrative monthly cloud budget of US$80. Your assignment is to recommend a **safe and realistic architecture** with no actual purchases required.

## Submission requirements

1. Write a requirements brief: functional needs, availability, recovery expectations, privacy and operating capacity.
2. Compare two service models and select one with evidence and trade-offs.
3. Draw a labelled logical architecture showing users, public endpoint, app compute, private data, admin identity and monitoring.
4. Explain cloud provider/customer responsibilities and apply least privilege to at least three roles.
5. Build a cost estimate from explicit unit prices, volume assumptions, region and a traffic increase scenario; include a budget alert.
6. Write RTO and RPO targets and a tested/simulated backup-and-recovery method.
7. Design three positive and three negative tests for access, cost and health/recovery behaviour.
8. Present a short rollout checklist, safe rollback, support ownership and a 200-word executive recommendation.

## Marking rubric

| Competency | Points | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Needs, constraints and model choice | 15 | Traceable criteria and logical trade-offs |
| Architecture and access controls | 25 | Clear diagram, data protection and responsibility |
| Cost, monitoring and recovery | 25 | Accurate assumptions, thresholds, RTO/RPO and rollback |
| Positive and negative validation | 20 | Six reproducible scenarios, including denied access |
| Documentation and decision quality | 15 | Clear owner, decision record and reflection |
| **Passing mark** | **70/100** | **Every critical condition must pass** |

**Critical rules:** no uncontrolled billable deployment, exposed credentials or real client files; deny anonymous access to private data; no unsupported SLA/security claims. Only a competent assessor can verify course completion.
