# Capstone: cloud-ready training business

## Scenario

A fictional training provider wants a web-accessible course catalogue, secure student progress data, predictable monthly costs and a tested incident procedure. You are hired to create an implementation-ready *proposal and simulation*, not to deploy billable systems.

## Deliverable pack

1. Record stakeholders, business scope, user journeys, RTO/RPO targets and sample demand.
2. Compare IaaS, PaaS and SaaS and select a model with clear provider/customer responsibility boundaries.
3. Draw public entry, DNS/TLS, app logic, identity, private data store, object storage, logging and backup.
4. Prepare a twelve-month illustrative budget using stated unit-rate assumptions and both baseline and peak demand.
5. Implement a four-role access matrix and show one authorised request and one denied request.
6. Develop three success and three negative test cases including credential misuse, spending limits and regional failure.
7. Write a monitoring/incident runbook with on-call ownership, alert thresholds, rollback and restore drill.
8. Summarise risks, assumptions and the next controlled deployment decision for management.

## Scoring rubric

| Domain | Maximum points | Full-credit criterion |
|---|---:|---|
| Business requirements and model choice | 15 | Explicit user needs and service responsibility allocation |
| Architecture, governance and least privilege | 25 | Clear boundaries and sensitive data protection |
| Budget, scale and recovery design | 25 | Checked calculations, RTO/RPO and realistic failure planning |
| Tested functional/security behaviours | 20 | Six cases with observable results and corrective actions |
| Operational documentation and reflection | 15 | Monitoring, incident ownership and recovery runbook |
| **Total** | **100** | **70/100 plus all critical security criteria** |

**Critical rules:** no live secrets, no unauthorised/billable provisioning, no public access to private data, no unsupported pricing guarantees, and no release without monitoring/recovery ownership. Failure of a critical rule requires remediation even if the numeric score is 70 or above. An authorised human assessor must verify evidence and record the decision before issuing any Academy completion award.
