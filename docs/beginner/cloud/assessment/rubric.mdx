# Assessment and evidence policy

**Knowledge:** at least 10/12. **Capstone:** at least 70/100, with all critical safety rules met. Learners should supply independent evidence that the design protects sensitive resources and that budget assumptions are transparent.

| Evidence domain | Points | Typical failure |
|---|---:|---|
| Scope and business requirements | 15 | No owners or success criteria |
| Service selection and security architecture | 25 | Publicly reachable private database |
| Cost and continuity | 25 | Unsupported rates or untested recovery |
| Positive and negative tests | 20 | No evidence of denied access or failures |
| Monitoring, incident management and reflection | 15 | No escalation or rollback owner |

Assessor records criterion-level marks, not only a total. Two guided retries may be attempted using equivalent scenarios after targeted feedback. Browser progress and diagnostic storage are formative. Protect submission evidence with minimum-retention, role-based access controls; do not place API keys or confidential client data in public repos.
