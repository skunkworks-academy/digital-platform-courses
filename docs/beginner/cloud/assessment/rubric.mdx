# Competency and grading requirements

Knowledge: **10/12**. Capstone: **70/100** with all mandatory safety, access and financial controls. Labs: three reproducible written or simulated practical submissions including positive and denied test cases.

| Operability and reflection | 15 | — | — | — |

Provide criterion-level feedback and allow up to two guided attempts. Require safe, redacted student evidence. A browser self-check or progress percentage is not an official pass decision.
