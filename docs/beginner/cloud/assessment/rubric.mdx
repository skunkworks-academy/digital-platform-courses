# Skills-based assessment policy

**Knowledge:** 10 out of 12 or higher. **Capstone:** 70 out of 100 or higher and pass on critical controls. Formal achievement needs authenticated learner enrolment, practical evidence and independent human moderation.

| Criterion | Marks | Typical development feedback |
|---|---:|---|
| Needs and decision model | 15 | State constraints and why the alternative was rejected |
| Secure architecture | 25 | Show identity and public/private boundaries |
| Cost, availability and recovery | 25 | Quantify assumptions and test restore requirements |
| Safe verification | 20 | Include failure, denied-access and exceeded-budget cases |
| Stakeholder communication | 15 | Explain trade-offs in nontechnical language |

Provide feedback at the criterion level and up to two guided attempts using equivalent scenarios; avoid storing keys or private records in evidence.
