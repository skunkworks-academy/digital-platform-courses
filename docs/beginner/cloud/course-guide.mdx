# Cloud Computing Fundamentals — Course guide

**Audience:** Curious beginners, early-career IT learners and business staff who evaluate cloud services. **Prerequisites:** a browser and basic awareness of websites and computers. Cloud accounts are optional, not required. Use only fictional data and price assumptions in labs.

## Learning sequence

1. Take the five-item **before** learning diagnostic.
2. Complete each of the six lessons and keep your diagrams, calculations and reflection answers.
3. Complete three labs with one positive and one negative validation test each.
4. Attempt the twelve-question final knowledge check without viewing the key.
5. Submit the capstone design against the published rubric and seek human assessor feedback for verified achievement.
6. Take the five-item **after** diagnostic and describe what improved.

**Knowledge standard:** at least 10/12 (83.3%), meeting the 80% minimum. **Capstone standard:** at least 70/100 plus all critical safety controls. Browser progress is local self-assessment only; it is not an authenticated Academy transcript.

## Accessibility and technical safety

All activities can be performed on paper or in a simple editor. You do not need a paid cloud subscription, credentials or admin access. Provide a text description of diagrams, simple tables and clear citations for any sourced claims. Never include live API keys, customer data or real prices without current provider verification.
