# Guide for learners

**Course code:** SKW-BEG-CLD-101. **Duration:** 6–8 hours. **Audience:** IT beginners, small-business decision makers, junior service-desk staff and future cloud practitioners.

## Suggested learning sequence

1. Complete the five-question **Before learning** self-check, without referring to content.
2. Read the six modules in order and perform their simulated activities.
3. Complete three practical labs. Use a spreadsheet or written evidence pack to record assumptions, tests and changes.
4. Answer the 12-question final knowledge assessment; a pass is at least **10/12**.
5. Complete the capstone project with at least **70/100** and all security-critical conditions passed.
6. Repeat the diagnostic after learning, compare both scores and identify one next skill to build.

## Safe practical environment

This course does not require a paid cloud account. Use paper architecture diagrams and a cost spreadsheet if accounts, credit cards or billing permissions are unavailable. If you elect to use a provider free tier, obtain permission from the billing owner, set alerts, inspect free-tier terms, never place real customer data and clean up resources at the end. Use temporary training identities and least privilege.

## Evidence standard

Record requirements, diagram legend, configuration assumptions, calculation sources and units, positive and negative test outcomes, rollback and a short reflection. Evidence remains local until an authorised Academy submission workflow is configured. Do not submit cloud credentials or secrets.

The published written assessments are formative; official grading, verified learner identity, certificate and payment fulfilment require the Academy portal and assessor. Support: [training@skunkworks.africa](mailto:training@skunkworks.africa).
