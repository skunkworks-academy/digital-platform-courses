# Lab 2: Cost calculator and governance exercise

**Estimated time:** 80 minutes. **Environment:** Offline spreadsheet with synthetic hourly rates, storage quantities and egress assumptions.

## Procedure

1. Calculate 100-hour versus 730-hour compute costs at an assumed unit rate.
2. Add storage, transfer and backup costs with clear units and a 20% usage sensitivity.
3. Define a project tag, billing owner, monthly threshold, action when reached and spending cap if supported.
4. Estimate one optimization such as shutting down nonproduction compute.
5. Explain that the calculation is illustrative and confirm live prices from a region-specific vendor calculator.

## Evidence of capability

**Acceptance criteria:** All numeric results reproduce and every cost assumption has an owner and review condition.

Produce a labelled diagram, worksheet and short decision record with assumptions, quantitative measures where possible, one successful and one denied/failed test, remediation and clear cost implications. Use fictitious systems and avoid actual billable deployments unless they are separately authorised and controlled.

## Quality and self-reflection

Can a second person reproduce your design and verify the result? What remains a single point of failure? How would you know if the assumptions became invalid or the monthly budget was exceeded?
