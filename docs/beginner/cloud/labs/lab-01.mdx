# Lab 1: Select a cloud architecture

**Estimated time:** 70 minutes. **Environment:** Fictional organisation with a basic website, private customer documents and a small budget.

## Procedure

1. Write functional and nonfunctional requirements with three measurable success criteria.
2. Compare SaaS, PaaS and IaaS by operations, resilience and cost for the website.
3. Draw the chosen service/data/identity diagram including public and private boundaries.
4. List provider/customer responsibilities and identify three failure modes.
5. Document one architecture alternative and why it was not selected.

## Evidence of capability

**Acceptance criteria:** Clear architecture decision grounded in business needs and explicitly bounded cost/security assumptions.

Produce a labelled diagram, worksheet and short decision record with assumptions, quantitative measures where possible, one successful and one denied/failed test, remediation and clear cost implications. Use fictitious systems and avoid actual billable deployments unless they are separately authorised and controlled.

## Quality and self-reflection

Can a second person reproduce your design and verify the result? What remains a single point of failure? How would you know if the assumptions became invalid or the monthly budget was exceeded?
