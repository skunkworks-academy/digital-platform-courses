# Lab 1: Choose a cloud architecture for a small organisation

**Duration:** 60 minutes. **Environment:** offline design or permitted free simulation; avoid live billable resources.

## Objective

Translate business needs into a simple service-model and regional recommendation.

## Steps

1. Read three fictional workloads: staff email, public brochure website and custom customer database.
2. Classify each as SaaS, PaaS or IaaS and record what the customer must still manage.
3. Choose a region and network boundary based on invented location and confidentiality rules.
4. Draw a component diagram including staff, provider and approval owner.
5. Write one alternative and explain its cost, complexity and risk.

## Evidence and acceptance

Service-model responsibility matrix, annotated diagram, assumptions and one-page recommendation.

**Pass criterion:** The proposal reflects legal/data needs, avoids unrestricted public database exposure and states trade-offs.

Create a test record showing intended action, allowed response, deliberately invalid/unauthorised input and observed or simulated refusal. Distinguish simulated output from actual provider observations. Document a correction and one residual risk.
