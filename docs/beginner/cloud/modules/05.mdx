# Module 5: Cloud pricing, scale, reliability and recovery

**Suggested time:** 45–70 minutes. **Competency:** Budget totals reconcile, hidden cost sources are acknowledged and recovery objectives align with data value.

## Concepts and architecture

Cloud costs can combine compute runtime, storage capacity, input/output operations, network egress, managed services, licences and support. A free-tier listing is not a permanent price guarantee. Compare total cost of ownership, including administration, backup, logging and unexpected resource growth. Autoscaling adjusts capacity to demand but requires quotas, application compatibility, observability and cost limits. A service-level agreement (SLA) defines a contractual availability target, not a promise of zero outages. Recovery Time Objective (RTO) is the longest acceptable recovery time; Recovery Point Objective (RPO) is the maximum tolerable data-loss window. Backups do not provide a recovery solution until restores are tested.

## Workplace scenario

A training site estimates 100 active users initially and 1,000 during an exam event. Forecast bandwidth and service capacity, then decide what to monitor and which resources require strict budget alerts.

## Applied exercise

1. Create a monthly budget with example unit assumptions for compute, storage, requests, outbound bandwidth, backups and support.
2. Calculate normal and peak-demand scenarios and state that simulated prices are not vendor quotes.
3. Set an example monthly budget threshold and an alert escalation owner.
4. Assign RTO and RPO to a public marketing page versus student assessment records, explaining differences.
5. Plan a restore test and document which component failure the recovery approach does not cover.

## Validation and reflection

Budget totals reconcile, hidden cost sources are acknowledged and recovery objectives align with data value. Create a verification register with **expected state**, **observed state**, **evidence**, **risk**, **owner** and **follow-up**. At least one test must demonstrate permitted activity and another must show an improper action is blocked or a faulty assumption is detected. Do not perform tests on systems without permission.

### Review questions

2. Why can a small cloud bill increase after a traffic spike?
