# Assessment rubric and moderation policy

**Evidence sources:** module artefacts, three labs, knowledge-check score and capstone evidence. **Knowledge:** minimum 10/12. **Capstone:** 70/100 and a pass on every critical criterion. **Feedback:** show specific corrections tied to each observed defect. **Retake:** provide up to two guided corrective attempts with equivalent difficulty.

| Rubric domain | Available points | Failure example |
|---|---:|---|
| Scope and learning outcomes | 15 | No target or baseline |
| Correct design and responsible use | 25 | No approval or privacy boundary |
| Verified output and documentation | 25 | Unsupported numeric or factual claim |
| Positive and negative tests | 20 | No failure injection or reproduction |
| Sustainability and reflection | 15 | No support owner or lessons learned |

An authorised human reviewer must moderate evidence. The learner may self-assess for formative feedback but cannot self-issue an official Academy award. Collect minimal assessment data and never retain secrets or unnecessary personal information.
