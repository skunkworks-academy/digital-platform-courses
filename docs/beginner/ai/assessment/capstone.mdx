# Final capstone: responsible AI productivity portfolio

## Client brief

A fictional, five-person training company wants to reduce the time spent on enquires, meeting notes and weekly reporting without weakening privacy or publishing inaccurate statements. Your task is to develop a repeatable **human-reviewed AI productivity workflow** that the company could trial without production personal data.

## Required work products

1. Describe the current process and measurable business goal, including time spent before and after.
2. Produce three improved prompts, their synthetic inputs and a template for human approval.
3. Transform meeting notes into an accurate action register, explicitly flagging missing dates and owners.
4. Summarise a five-row simulated dataset; verify totals and identify a data-quality problem.
5. Create three positive and three negative tests, including one prompt-injection attempt and one unsupported-claim check.
6. Prepare a short staff usage guideline covering data classification, permitted input, verification, ownership and escalation.
7. Write a one-page management recommendation with expected benefits, limitations and follow-up monitoring.

## Analytic scoring rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Full-credit demonstration |
|---|---:|---|
| Problem definition and measurable baseline | 15 | Realistic scope, audience, input boundaries and outcome metrics |
| Prompt and workflow quality | 25 | Structured reusable prompts, review gates and clear ownership |
| Correctness and verification | 25 | All numerical/factual claims checked and traceable |
| Positive and negative testing | 20 | At least six tests covering faults, missing facts and injection |
| Operations, ethics and reflection | 15 | Privacy, accountability, support and evidence of improvement |
| **Total** | **100** | **At least 70, plus all critical safety checks** |

**Critical checks:** no private credentials or customer records; no invented facts published as verified; no unauthorised automated sending; all high-impact actions require human approval.

A competent assessor records criterion scores and gives targeted feedback. Retakes should use a comparable new scenario. Completing a public quiz or progress tracker alone does not issue a verified credential.
