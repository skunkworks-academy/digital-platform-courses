# Generative AI & ChatGPT for Beginners

**SKW-BEG-AI-101 · Beginner · 6–8 hours · Self-paced · proposed USD 50**

Use generative AI to draft, analyse and improve everyday work while verifying accuracy, protecting sensitive information and keeping people accountable.

**Audience:** Office workers, students, job seekers and small-business owners with basic web-browsing skills.

**Prerequisites:** A modern browser, sample fictional documents and access to an approved AI service or the supplied paper-based prompt exercises. No programming required.

## What you will accomplish

1. Understanding AI and what it can do: A credible table distinguishes generation from factual verification and names a person responsible for publishing or acting.
2. Writing prompts that produce useful results: The improved prompt should reduce unsupported assumptions, make missing facts visible and produce a format that is easy to review.
3. Fact-checking and responsible research: A corrected brief must clearly separate observations, assumptions, and unsupported claims; every consequential fact has traceable evidence.
4. Documents, summaries and communication: The output retains all essential facts and never invents task ownership, deadlines or approvals.
5. Working with data and simple calculations: The arithmetic reconciles, identified data-quality errors have documented treatment, and the narrative avoids causal claims unsupported by data.
6. Building a safe personal AI workflow: The workflow names a human owner, evidence, exception path, privacy boundary and measurable improvement target.

## Your learning pathway

Begin with the baseline check, work through the six lessons in order, perform all three practical labs, take the 12-question knowledge assessment and submit a capstone portfolio. Return to the diagnostic after finishing and compare your understanding. Allow about 6–8 hours including practical work; learners who are new to the tools may need longer.
