# Course guide

**Course ID:** SKW-BEG-AI-101  
**Level:** Beginner. **Duration:** 6–8 hours. **Estimated learning mode:** independent study with self-assessment and practical evidence.  
**Audience:** Office workers, students, job seekers and small-business owners with basic web-browsing skills.

## Before you start

A modern browser, sample fictional documents and access to an approved AI service or the supplied paper-based prompt exercises. No programming required. Use only data and systems you own or are explicitly authorised to use. Every task provides a simulation option when paid software is unavailable. Do not upload real customer documents, secrets or regulated data.

## Study plan

1. Complete the five-question **before learning** diagnostic on the course home page.
2. Work through all six modules. First read the explanation; then follow the step-by-step applied task. Complete the two module self-check questions in writing before comparing your response with the capability criterion.
3. Complete three applied labs. Produce the artefacts stated in each lab and test both a successful and a deliberately invalid input.
4. Attempt the twelve-question end-of-course knowledge check without expanding its answers.
5. Produce the capstone evidence pack, apply the rubric and seek independent review.
6. Repeat the five-question **after learning** diagnostic and document areas of growth and next steps.

## Achievement and grading

The knowledge assessment pass mark is **at least 10/12 (83.3%)** to meet the minimum 80% target. The capstone needs at least 70/100 and must satisfy all safety-critical criteria. The browser diagnostics and progress checklist are formative only; they are not official completion records. An Academy certificate or verified badge needs a separately recorded, authenticated assessment and competent human moderation.

## Accessibility

Written alternatives are provided for every exercise. Use headings, tables and diagrams with text descriptions. Operate navigational links and answers with a keyboard; learners can enlarge browser text. Ask [Academy training support](mailto:training@skunkworks.africa) about alternative evidence formats.

## Evidence standard

Provide the task brief, the input (redacted or fictional), final artefact, at least one positive and one negative test, your corrections, the date, and a 100-word reflection. Do not include passwords or identity documents. Save locally until the Academy provides an approved authenticated submission channel.
