# Lab 3: AI-assisted business analysis with verification

**Estimated time:** 80 minutes. **Practical type:** independently repeatable.

## Scenario and acceptance brief

Use a tiny fictional dataset to produce a verifiable business observation and recommend a safe workflow improvement.

**Materials:** A CSV with five synthetic rows; any spreadsheet application.

## Procedure

1. Calculate totals and averages by deterministic formulas, identifying one duplicate and one missing record.
2. Ask AI for two possible interpretations, then separate observations from assumptions.
3. Verify the numbers independently and document an uncertainty or limitation.
4. Produce a decision log with evidence URL or worksheet cell references.
5. Present an executive recommendation with an explicit human approval step.

## Verification plan

Record the intended output, a clean input that should succeed, a missing or hostile input that should be denied or flagged, actual results, and any remediation. The learner must be able to explain why the approach is safe rather than just stating that it worked.

**Pass condition:** All numeric findings reconcile and a reviewer can reproduce them without relying on an AI assertion.

## Deliverables and evidence

Submit a Markdown/PDF report or accessible document, a redacted screenshot or text log, relevant calculations, and a personal reflection of 100–150 words. Avoid real customer information. Keep all evidence in an authorised private location; public GitHub issues and repository commits are not private evidence storage.

## Troubleshooting checkpoint

If a result differs from your plan, isolate the input, tool setting, permission, data condition or evaluation rule. Change one factor, retest and document the difference. Do not bypass a safety rule to make the test pass.
