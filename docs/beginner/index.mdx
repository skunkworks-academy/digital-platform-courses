## How a learner proves progress

1. Start with an ungraded baseline knowledge diagnostic.
2. Complete six lessons, their checkpoints and three applied practical labs.
3. Attempt a 12-question interactive knowledge quiz with immediate results.
4. Prepare a workplace-style capstone evidence pack and evaluate against its published rubric.
5. Repeat the diagnostic and record measurable skills growth.
