# Cybersecurity Essentials & Online Safety

**SKW-BEG-SEC-101 · Beginner · 6–8 hours · self-paced · proposed US$50**

Recognise common cyber risks, protect accounts and devices, identify phishing, and respond to security incidents using practical low-risk procedures.

**Audience:** First-time IT learners, employees, parents, students and small businesses. **Prerequisites:** A browser, an authorised computer or phone and a text editor; all simulations use fake data.

## Outcomes

1. Cyber threats, assets and everyday risk: A risk register exists with asset, event, likelihood, consequence, owner, control and residual risk.
2. Passwords, passkeys and multifactor authentication: Every fictional service has unique credentials and a recorded secondary sign-in/recovery method.
3. Detecting phishing, scams and malicious links: All dangerous examples are flagged without clicking links or opening attachments; a clear reporting path is recorded.
4. Securing phones, computers and home networks: The plan reduces easy account/device compromise, limits guest exposure and proves at least one recoverable test file.
5. Safe browsing, privacy and data handling: A data handling table defines purpose, owner, recipient, authorised location and review/removal date.
6. Responding to incidents and building good habits: The response is timely, avoids exposing credentials, escalates appropriately and documents recovery verification.

## Begin your learning journey

Take the ungraded five-question baseline, work through six lessons, complete three practical exercises, take the twelve-question final knowledge assessment, assemble the capstone and repeat the diagnostic to evaluate learning growth. Use the progress checklist to monitor what you have studied.
