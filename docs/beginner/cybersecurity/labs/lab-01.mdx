# Lab 1: Phishing inbox triage

**Duration:** 55 minutes. **Environment:** sandbox, owned device or paper simulation.

## Business scenario

Review ten fictional emails and SMS messages without opening links, attachments or executing code.

## Practical steps

1. Group messages by claimed sender, urgent request, link domain and attachment indicator.
2. Mark each as plausible, suspicious or malicious and justify the classification.
3. Prepare a safe verification method for at least three suspicious messages.
4. Produce a one-page staff awareness card explaining how and where to report.
5. Include a false-positive example and explain how you would avoid misclassifying a legitimate message.

## Evidence package

A 10-row triage worksheet and a reporting message with indicators and timestamp. Include timestamp, expected state, actual state and a reflection on any discrepancies. Use fictitious cases; do not collect private credentials or customer records.

## Acceptance criteria

All simulated credential-harvest and urgent payment scenarios are stopped or independently verified.

## Troubleshooting challenge

Run a deliberately invalid or missing-input scenario, record the failure, determine the safest next step and retest after correction. Do not bypass controls or alter live systems for this exercise.
