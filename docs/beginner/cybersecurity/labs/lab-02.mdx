# Lab 2: Personal device and account hardening

**Duration:** 65 minutes. **Environment:** sandbox, owned device or paper simulation.

## Business scenario

Create a reproducible hardening procedure on an owned test device or offline checklist.

## Practical steps

1. Document screen lock, software updates, encryption, backup and MFA readiness.
2. Choose one hypothetical weakness and demonstrate a remediation or simulated configuration change.
3. Test restoration of one non-sensitive sample file.
4. Write a lost-device and lost-passkey recovery procedure.
5. Record least-privilege and privacy review decisions.

## Evidence package

Before/after settings matrix with redacted evidence, recovery procedure and successful sample restore. Include timestamp, expected state, actual state and a reflection on any discrepancies. Use fictitious cases; do not collect private credentials or customer records.

## Acceptance criteria

Device risks are reduced without sharing secrets or affecting unauthorised devices.

## Troubleshooting challenge

Run a deliberately invalid or missing-input scenario, record the failure, determine the safest next step and retest after correction. Do not bypass controls or alter live systems for this exercise.
