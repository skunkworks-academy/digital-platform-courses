# Lab 3: Small-business incident tabletop

**Duration:** 75 minutes. **Environment:** sandbox, owned device or paper simulation.

## Business scenario

Respond to a fictional scam that compromises a staff account and sends an unauthorised invoice.

## Practical steps

1. Build an event timeline based on eight supplied fictional facts.
2. Identify affected assets, potential impact and first safe containment action.
3. Draft an internal escalation note and verified customer communication draft.
4. Define account recovery, payment verification, audit and monitoring steps.
5. Run an alternative scenario where the email was a false alarm and document the changed decision.

## Evidence package

Incident ticket, timeline, role assignments, recovery checklist and retrospective. Include timestamp, expected state, actual state and a reflection on any discrepancies. Use fictitious cases; do not collect private credentials or customer records.

## Acceptance criteria

Containment is proportionate, evidence is preserved, and sensitive actions are reserved for authorised staff.

## Troubleshooting challenge

Run a deliberately invalid or missing-input scenario, record the failure, determine the safest next step and retest after correction. Do not bypass controls or alter live systems for this exercise.
