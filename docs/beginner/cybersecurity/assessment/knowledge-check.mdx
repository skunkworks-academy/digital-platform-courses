<details>
  <summary>Answer key and feedback — reveal after attempting</summary>

1. **A. Confidentiality, integrity and availability** — CIA describes three fundamental protection goals.

2. **A. A system weakness that could be exploited** — Weakness and threat are different.

3. **B. Unique passwords and suitable MFA** — Unique credentials reduce cross-service compromise.

4. **B. Unexpected urgent payment request using a shortened link** — Urgency and unexpected payment links require verification.

5. **B. No, it protects transport not business legitimacy** — HTTPS doesn't validate a seller's ethics.

6. **B. Update firmware and secure management login** — Harden administration and keep firmware supported.

7. **B. Report without opening it** — Avoid exposure and report safely.

8. **B. Access is limited to what the role requires** — Reduce exposure and blast radius.

9. **B. Test restoring representative files** — Recovery requires verified restore capability.

10. **B. Report, use the official site to reset and review sessions** — Contain and investigate via authorised channels.

11. **B. Use approved storage with minimum permissions** — Purpose-limited, authorised sharing reduces risk.

12. **A. To support investigation and recovery decisions** — A timeline preserves critical evidence.
