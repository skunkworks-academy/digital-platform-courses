# Course guide

**Code:** SKW-BEG-SEC-101  
**Duration:** 6–8 hours · **Level:** Beginner · **Method:** self-paced, evidence-led.  
**Audience:** First-time IT learners, employees, parents, students and small businesses.

## Prerequisites

A browser, an authorised computer or phone and a text editor; all simulations use fake data. Use only synthetic information and equipment on which you have approval to practise. No real customer data, passwords or payment credentials belong in submissions.

## Recommended route

1. Start the baseline five-question diagnostic on the course home page.
2. Read six lessons and perform their guided tasks with expected/actual results recorded.
3. Complete each practical lab, including a deliberate negative case and corrective action.
4. Take the final 12-question knowledge check without consulting the key.
5. Assemble a capstone evidence pack and self-assess against the rubric; obtain competent human review for verified completion.
6. Repeat the diagnostic after studying; document gaps and what you'll practise next.

## Assessment thresholds

Knowledge check: **at least 10/12 (83.3%)**, satisfying the minimum 80% target. Capstone: **70/100 plus all critical safety criteria**. Lab evidence must be reproducible. A self-reported browser score is not an official record or certificate.

## Accessible study options

Every task offers a written simulation route where a tool or licence is unavailable. Use clear headings and readable tables, captions or text descriptions for visuals, and keyboard-accessible navigation. Contact [Academy support](mailto:training@skunkworks.africa) for reasonable accommodations.

## What to save as evidence

Record task title, synthetic inputs, steps, expected and observed outputs, a successful and failed test, correction and a brief reflection. Store locally or in an approved private destination; public GitHub is not a private student portfolio.
