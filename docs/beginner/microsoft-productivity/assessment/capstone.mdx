# Final capstone: Digital office productivity pack

## Brief

A fictional 15-person training firm wants an accurate monthly training summary for directors, collaborative project notes for staff and a controlled document shared with a subcontractor. Create a coherent office productivity portfolio without any real learner information.

## Deliverables

1. Design a simple file/folder and sharing policy for staff, managers and the external subcontractor.
2. Produce a one-page Word-style management report with semantic headings, risks and next actions.
3. Build an Excel worksheet with twelve synthetic transactions, computed totals and at least one IF or COUNTIF formula.
4. Demonstrate relative versus absolute references, a detected duplicate and a missing-value test.
5. Build a correctly labelled chart and explain the metric's data source, period and interpretation.
6. Prepare accessible Teams meeting minutes, action-owner table and a concise Outlook invitation.
7. Supply at least three positive and three negative quality/security test cases with evidence.
8. Draft a final review-and-publication checklist, naming who validates calculations and approves external sharing.

## Rubric

| Competency | Points | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Requirements and information ownership | 15 | Clear audience, file location, access and deadlines |
| Spreadsheet/formula quality | 25 | Correct formulas, references, errors and data validation |
| Document and collaboration design | 25 | Accessible content, controlled sharing and meeting actions |
| Validation and negative tests | 20 | Six objectively demonstrated tests |
| Reproducibility and professional communication | 15 | Clear instructions and reflection |
| **Total** | **100** | **70 or more and no critical safety failures** |

**Mandatory:** no real personal/customer financial records; no public sharing of confidential documents; no financial totals reported without checking source values. Instructor/assessor moderation is required before verified Academy completion.
