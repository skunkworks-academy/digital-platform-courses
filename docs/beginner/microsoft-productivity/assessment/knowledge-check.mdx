  <summary>Show answers and learning feedback</summary>

1. **A — Personal or individually owned cloud files with controlled sharing**. OneDrive stores and shares authorised files.

2. **A — With semantic heading styles**. Heading styles give structure for navigation.

3. **A — =**. The equals sign introduces a calculation.

4. **B — $B$1**. Dollar markers fix row and column.

5. **A — Total of numeric cells in range**. SUM adds numeric values.

6. **A — Records can become misaligned**. Keep rows together when sorting.

7. **A — Summarising and grouping structured data**. PivotTables aggregate records.

8. **A — To make measures understandable and prevent misreading**. Context is essential for accurate interpretation.

9. **A — Recipients visible as copied**. Cc sends visible copies.

10. **A — Specific people**. Specific-person links restrict access.

11. **A — A documented metric with source and timeframe**. KPIs require consistent scope.

12. **A — Check recipients, facts, formulas and approval**. Review reduces errors and disclosure risk.
