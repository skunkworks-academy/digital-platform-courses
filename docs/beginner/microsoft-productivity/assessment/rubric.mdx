# Competency rubric

**Knowledge threshold:** 10/12. **Capstone:** 70/100 plus mandatory privacy/integrity checks. Authentic laboratory evidence and a competent reviewer are required for a verified completion award.

| Criterion | Points | Typical correction |
|---|---:|---|
| Requirements and access ownership | 15 | Assign owners and intended recipients |
| Workbook and formula accuracy | 25 | Recheck references, totals, duplicates |
| Accessibility and shared workflow | 25 | Use heading styles and least-privilege links |
| Safe positive and negative testing | 20 | Add error and denied-access tests |
| Communication and reflection | 15 | Explain limitations and support path |

The assessor records per-criterion feedback and offers up to two guided equivalent resubmissions. Do not retain more learner evidence than approved by Academy policy.
