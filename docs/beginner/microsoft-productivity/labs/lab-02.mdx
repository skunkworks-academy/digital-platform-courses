# Lab 2: Write and share an accessible project update

**Approximate time:** 70 minutes. **Environment:** Use a fictional training project and any approved word processor or document simulation.

## Steps

1. Produce a concise one-page status report with semantic headings and descriptive links.
2. Create meeting minutes containing decisions, owner, deadline and unresolved question.
3. Design a file-sharing matrix for internal, external and confidential versions.
4. Describe an approved share and revocation check, without publicly publishing sensitive documents.
5. Draft an Outlook/Teams invitation with agenda, time zone and recipient review.

## Acceptance criteria

Professional document and message with an explicit restricted-sharing design and clear next actions.

## Evidence checklist

Provide the editable workbook or text document (with fictional data), a screenshot or exported view, one normal scenario, one erroneous/denied scenario and an explanation of how the final artefact can be maintained. Redact personal or privileged information and document owner/approval.

## Self-review

Check arithmetic independently; verify chart units and table sources; and make sure external-sharing assumptions are explicit rather than implied by a successful save action.
