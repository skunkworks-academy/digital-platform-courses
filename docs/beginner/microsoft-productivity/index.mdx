# Microsoft 365 & Excel Productivity Essentials

**SKW-BEG-M365-101 · Beginner · 8–10 hours · Self-paced · proposed US$50**

Create professional documents, collaborate safely and analyse simple business data with Microsoft 365 and Excel.

**Audience:** New office staff, students, administrators and small-business operators. **Prerequisites:** Access to Excel and Microsoft 365, compatible desktop/web apps, or a spreadsheet alternative for written practice; no administrative permissions needed.

## Course outcomes

1. **Getting started with Microsoft 365:** A fictional design agency has five staff, one shared training schedule and separate private finance records. Map which files should be personal, team-controlled and finance-restricted.
2. **Word, Outlook and everyday communication:** A manager asks for a one-page training announcement and an email invitation to seven employees. Draft an announcement with heading styles, accessible action text and a calendar invite with explicit time zone.
3. **Excel workbooks, formulas and references:** A shop records quantities 2, 4 and 3 with unit prices 50, 25 and 100. Calculate the line amounts 100, 100 and 300, and the total 500 using formulas, not manually overwritten results.
4. **Cleaning tables, sorting and analysis:** A fictional expense register has a duplicated receipt, mixed date formats and an empty category. Standardise values, flag duplicates, then compare category totals.
5. **Teams, OneDrive and collaborative work:** A four-person team is collaborating on a client proposal; one external reviewer needs access to only a single approved draft. Choose a limited share, define owner and an end date, and verify the reviewer cannot browse unrelated files.
6. **Productivity workflows and quality control:** A training team wants to report enquiries, enrolments and attendance each week. Build a small workbook that calculates totals and conversion rate without including personal student records.

## Your learning journey

Complete a baseline skills check, the six guided lessons, three practical labs, a 12-item knowledge test and a final capstone. Work with entirely fictional or personally authorised files. Compare the same skills check at the end; self-reported scores and checklists are not formal Academy transcript data.
