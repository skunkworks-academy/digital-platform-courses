# Lab 2: Wi-Fi and DNS troubleshooting

**Duration:** 75 minutes. **Environment:** sandbox, owned device or paper simulation.

## Business scenario

Isolate network symptoms across device, access point, local gateway, DNS resolver and remote service.

## Practical steps

1. Review three fictional device connectivity reports and identify which share the same failure point.
2. Interpret IPv4 address, prefix, gateway and DNS details.
3. Write a test sequence from local link to external application.
4. Inject a simulated DNS failure, compare it with loss of Wi-Fi authentication and record how symptoms differ.
5. Produce a clear flowchart and escalation decision.

## Evidence package

Network decision flow, command outputs (simulated or authorised) and before/after ticket notes. Include timestamp, expected state, actual state and a reflection on any discrepancies. Use fictitious cases; do not collect private credentials or customer records.

## Acceptance criteria

Testing is layered, safe and reproducible; cause remains 'unconfirmed' until supported by results.

## Troubleshooting challenge

Run a deliberately invalid or missing-input scenario, record the failure, determine the safest next step and retest after correction. Do not bypass controls or alter live systems for this exercise.
