# Lab 1: Slow-laptop diagnostic case

**Duration:** 75 minutes. **Environment:** sandbox, owned device or paper simulation.

## Business scenario

Triage a fictional laptop with slow startup, limited storage and many browser processes.

## Practical steps

1. Capture operating-system version, CPU and memory usage, free disk space and startup application count from synthetic output.
2. Generate at least three hypotheses and rank them by likelihood and reversibility.
3. Run or simulate low-risk tests before proposing any change.
4. Create an action plan and separate temporary workaround from lasting remediation.
5. Record before/after evidence and user confirmation.

## Evidence package

A timestamped ticket, diagnostic matrix, action/rollback plan and customer update. Include timestamp, expected state, actual state and a reflection on any discrepancies. Use fictitious cases; do not collect private credentials or customer records.

## Acceptance criteria

Conclusions follow observed evidence, with no premature hardware purchase or unsafe deletion.

## Troubleshooting challenge

Run a deliberately invalid or missing-input scenario, record the failure, determine the safest next step and retest after correction. Do not bypass controls or alter live systems for this exercise.
