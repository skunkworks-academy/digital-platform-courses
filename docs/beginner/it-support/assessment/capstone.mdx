# Final capstone: applied beginner skills portfolio

## Scenario

A fictional ten-person community organisation is introducing responsible digital practices. You have been asked to evaluate its current problem, create a safe process, validate the result and train staff. Work within the subject of this course; use only permitted devices and invented records.

## Required deliverables

1. A one-page case definition explaining stakeholders, current pain points, impact, risks and measurable outcome.
2. A labelled process/architecture diagram or decision flow showing people, devices, tools and approval responsibilities.
3. Two practical procedures derived from the labs, with versioned screenshots, simulated outputs or logs.
4. Three positive cases and three negative cases with expected and actual outcomes.
5. A safety and privacy checklist, including authority boundaries and incident escalation.
6. A customer-friendly quick-start guide and a 150-word self-reflection on improved capability.

## Scoring rubric

| Criterion | Points | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Scope and outcomes | 15 | Correct problem statement, stakeholders and baseline |
| Correct technical design | 25 | Accurate concepts, permission boundaries and solution plan |
| Implementation and reproducibility | 25 | Clear procedures, realistic artefacts and verified results |
| Positive and negative testing | 20 | Six verifiable scenarios with controlled handling |
| Operations and reflection | 15 | Safe support hand-off, privacy and improvement plan |
| **Total** | **100** | **Minimum 70 plus all critical safety checks** |

**Critical criteria:** no unapproved access or scanning, no disclosure of private credentials or customer information, no unverified claim of success, and no irreversible changes without authorisation. A critical failure requires remediation regardless of score.

An authorised reviewer should give criterion-level feedback and permit up to two guided resubmissions using equivalent scenarios. Completing a local self-check is not a verified course certificate.
