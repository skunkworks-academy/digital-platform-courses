# Getting started

**SKW-BEG-CYB-101 · 6–8 hours · Beginner.** A smartphone or computer and a browser. All security exercises use simulated messages and authorized devices.

## Recommended order

1. Complete the ungraded baseline diagnostic on the home page.
2. Complete six lessons in order, responding to each two-question formative checkpoint.
3. Perform three independent practical labs and keep fictional/redacted proof.
4. Answer the 12-question final knowledge test before reading its key. A score of **10/12** meets the 80% threshold.
5. Submit the case-study capstone for rubric review: **70/100** and all critical security controls are needed.
6. Return to the home-page diagnostic and compare post-course results; create a personal improvement plan.

## Responsible practice

Never use phishing emails, malware samples or scanning of other people's networks. Keep real passwords, identity numbers, customer data, live messages and personal device serial numbers out of evidence files. When access is unavailable, use the provided fictional case and produce a reproducible decision record instead of changing live settings.

## Accessible assessment and evidence

Written reports, screenshots with alt-text, structured tables or audio descriptions of actions may be acceptable alternatives, subject to assessor confirmation. Each lab needs objective expected/actual results, a negative test and a remediation explanation. Record the learner's reference ID but do not publish real personal information.

**Completion claim:** only an authorised assessor can grant an Academy-branded achievement after evidence review. Browser diagnostics and progress markers alone do not issue a certificate. Contact [training support](mailto:training@skunkworks.africa).
