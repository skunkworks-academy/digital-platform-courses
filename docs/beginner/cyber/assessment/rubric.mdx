# Competency and evidence rubric

**Formative:** module prompts and baseline/final browser diagnostic. **Summative:** 12-question knowledge assessment (10/12 minimum) and practical capstone (70/100 minimum) plus all critical safety rules.

| Criterion | Points | Typical weakness |
|---|---:|---|
| Asset and risk reasoning | 15 | Risks stated without context or ownership |
| Preventive design | 25 | No MFA, shared identity or missing backup validation |
| Incident decisions | 25 | Evidence deleted, unsafe actions or unverifiable cause |
| Validated tests | 20 | Only happy path, no negative scenario |
| Communication and improvement | 15 | Inaccessible deliverable or no support process |

For every failed criterion, provide a documented improvement and up to two guided retake opportunities with equivalent scenarios. An independent competent reviewer signs off final competency. Keep sensitive evidence out of public repositories and use minimal retention policies.
