# Capstone: Protect a small business

## Scenario

A fictional 12-person service company uses Microsoft 365, a shared guest Wi-Fi network, supplier invoices and one shared front-desk device. A suspicious supplier-change email and multiple unexpected login prompts were reported. Prepare an **entry-level security improvement pack**; do not interact with real attackers.

## Deliverables

1. Asset and risk register: five assets, three threats, relevant impact and owners.
2. Account and device baseline: MFA recommendations, individual access, update and recovery controls.
3. Phishing-response decision tree, including independently verifying payment changes.
4. Guest Wi-Fi and remote-work trust boundary diagram with legend.
5. A safe incident-response record covering first report, evidence preservation, containment authority, recovery and improvement.
6. Three positive and three negative tests using synthetic scenarios only.
7. A one-page staff awareness bulletin and an executive summary prioritising three cost-effective controls.

## Scoring rubric

| Dimension | Maximum | Evidence for full credit |
|---|---:|---|
| Risk framing and prioritisation | 15 | Clear asset, threat, impact, probability and ownership |
| Controls and secure design | 25 | Appropriate MFA, device, backup, data and network safeguards |
| Incident response and communication | 25 | Safe escalation and evidence-preserving decisions |
| Positive/negative scenario tests | 20 | Six test records including denied or failed cases |
| Documentation and reflection | 15 | Accessible artefacts, improvement priorities and residual risks |
| **Total** | **100** | **70 minimum, with all critical checks passed** |

**Critical:** no live phishing tests, no malware execution, no third-party scanning, no exposed secrets, and no unsanctioned access. Failing a critical rule requires remediation regardless of score. A human assessor must verify evidence for official Academy completion.
