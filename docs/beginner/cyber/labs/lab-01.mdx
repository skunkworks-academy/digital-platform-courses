# Lab 1: Spot and report a phishing attempt

**Time:** 60 minutes. **Required environment:** Use five synthetic email/text examples with realistic indicators but fake links and addresses.

## Procedure

1. For each message, mark sender, domain, requested action, urgency and likely risk.
2. Choose verify/report/delete for each, explaining the independent verification channel.
3. Draft a short safe incident report with timestamp, evidence description and affected account.
4. Repeat with a benign but urgent business message and explain why context matters.
5. Demonstrate that you never click or forward a suspicious link.

## Assessment evidence

Create a short lab portfolio with the original scenario, purpose, initial conditions, approved scope, expected result, actual result, and a safe negative or failure test. Include one improvement you would make to the operating procedure.

**Acceptance:** At least four of five classifications are supported by a defensible rationale and all high-risk requests are verified outside the original message.

## Troubleshooting and reflection

If the scenario behaves unexpectedly, identify what new evidence you need to isolate the cause. Never disable account protection, run live phishing links or test third-party assets just to obtain a passing result. Keep evidence private, redact identifiers and justify the response.
