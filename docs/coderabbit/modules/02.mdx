# Installation, Privacy and Scope

**Outcome:** Produce an approved installation decision that limits repositories, permissions, and sensitive content.

Before installing, identify data classifications in source, diffs, issues, PR comments, logs, and connected knowledge systems. Review vendor terms, retention, model/data-use commitments, subprocessors, region, access controls, incident processes, and deletion options under your organization’s policy. Obtain the required security, privacy, legal, and repository-owner approvals.

Limit the GitHub App installation to selected repositories where possible. Review permissions and explain each material grant. Remove stale installations and access. Protect secrets before they reach a review: ignore files, use secret scanning, and keep generated logs free of credentials and personal data.

Establish boundaries in a short control record:

- approved repositories and excluded data classes;
- installation owner and periodic access review;
- allowed commands and code-generation behaviour;
- escalation route for unsafe or confidential output;
- removal, incident, and evidence-retention procedure.
