# Measurement, Tuning and Governance

**Outcome:** Evaluate review-system performance and propose a controlled configuration improvement.

Measure outcomes, not comment volume. A high count can mean excellent detection or distracting noise. Sample metrics:

- confirmed finding rate and false-positive rate by severity/path;
- median time from comment to verified resolution;
- escaped defects linked to reviewed changes;
- repeated finding rate after instructions or tests are added;
- reviewer/author usefulness rating;
- sensitive-output, access, or policy incidents;
- review coverage and service availability.

Sample findings monthly or per release. Calibrate reviewers on the same examples. Identify paths with noisy or missed feedback, then change one configuration element and compare a defined period. Preserve configuration diffs and decision rationale.

Review access, vendor changes, retention, model behaviour, and human override records periodically. Revalidate after new languages, architectures, sensitive data, or compliance obligations enter scope.
