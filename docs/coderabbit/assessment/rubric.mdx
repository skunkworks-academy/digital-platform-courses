# CodeRabbit Capstone Rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Meets standard | Critical? |
|---|---:|---|:---:|
| Access, privacy and ownership | 15 | Scope, permissions, data, owner, incident/removal are approved and bounded | Yes |
| Configuration validity | 20 | Current schema; precise triggers/exclusions/instructions; rationale | Yes |
| Human-owned workflow | 15 | AI is advisory within explicit tests, review, override and emergency controls | Yes |
| Triage quality | 20 | Claims classified, independently checked and dispositioned with evidence | Yes |
| Fix and verification | 10 | Correct change plus regression evidence and relevant validation | No |
| Measurement and tuning | 10 | Balanced measures, baseline, owner, experiment and rollback | No |
| Evidence integrity and explanation | 10 | Authentic, redacted, traceable and clearly defended | Yes |

Use 0/50/70/85/100% performance anchors. Pass at 70/100 and meet every critical criterion.
