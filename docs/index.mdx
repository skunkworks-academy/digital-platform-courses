## How it works

Work through guided lessons, complete practical exercises and take an interactive quiz. Track formative progress in your current browser. The Academy can only issue an official achievement after separately verifying an enrolment and assessing the learner's evidence.

**Public-course release:** Learning content is openly accessible. US$50 is a proposed future fee for managed enrolment/assessment, and no card payment is accepted by this preview. This is not an active automated-sales storefront.
