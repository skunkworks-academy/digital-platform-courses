## How the learning experience works

1. **Start at baseline.** Answer a five-question diagnostic to record what you know before training.
2. **Study six lessons.** Work through explanations, worked examples, tasks and feedback in order.
3. **Complete three practical labs.** Create real or simulated artefacts with positive and negative tests.
4. **Check mastery.** Answer twelve questions and complete the evidence-based capstone.
5. **Measure improvement.** Repeat the diagnostic and compare results; identify areas still requiring practice.

Knowledge checks target at least 80%; the capstone requires at least 70/100 and all critical security and privacy criteria. Browser diagnostic scores and lesson checklists are **self-reported and device-local**: they are not an authenticated transcript or certificate. A human-reviewed Academy certificate requires a separately recorded formal assessment.
